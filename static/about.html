What is Truth?

A companion on your journey to discover eternal truth through the restored gospel of Jesus Christ.

✦

Truth is an AI-powered companion for exploring the restored gospel of Jesus Christ. Ask questions about scripture, doctrine, and gospel principles, and receive answers grounded in the Book of Mormon, the Doctrine and Covenants, the Pearl of Great Price, and the Bible. Every answer you receive becomes a star in your own constellation — a record of the light you've gathered.

Grounded in scripture

Every answer is rooted in the standard works and the words of living prophets, with citations you can open and read yourself.

Seek, and ye shall find

Explore doctrine, history, and gospel principles with thoughtful, cited responses — and follow the questions wherever they lead.

Personal discovery

Find insights and connections across the scriptures that deepen your testimony and invite your own witness through the Holy Ghost.

A companion, not a replacement

Designed to complement personal study, prayer, and counsel with Church leaders — never to stand in for them.

By small and simple things

"By small and simple things are great things brought to pass." — Alma 37:6

Built with love, to invite all to come unto Christ.
